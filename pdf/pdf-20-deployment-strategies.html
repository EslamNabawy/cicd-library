03 / Failure Modes

  • Rolling without probes → bad version reaches 100% "successfully".
  • Blue/Green sharing mutable state → Green writes what Blue can't read; keep schemas backward-compatible.
  • Canary without version labels → blended metrics lie.
  • Flag sprawl → untestable combinations; every flag gets an owner + expiry.

04 / Interview Check

Q: Canary vs Rolling?
A: Rolling replaces capacity; canary tests a hypothesis on a subset with explicit success criteria.
Q: Why do stateful systems break strategy thinking?
A: Traffic shapes can't fix data: migrations must be expand-contract and backward-compatible first.
